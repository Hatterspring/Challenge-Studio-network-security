This is a replication of the commands, in order, that a Volt Typhoon style attack might feature. 
It is a pure LOTL attack, with no modules being installed during the attack and only relying on preinstalled 
software. Some rudimentary attempts at defense evasion, such as removing Indicators of Compromise are also made.

- The attack commences with a buffer overflow attack resulting in a reverse shell sent to the attacker's host machine. 
- Once initial access is gained, the shell is upgraded to allow job control. 
- then, vpn access credentials are gained from the firewall's log and domain controller credentials from an unsecured backup accessible from the admin's dashboard. 
- the admin's machine is then laterally moved to using SSH (instead of Volt Typhoon's usual RDP, which is not possible in Kathara). 
- From there, an unsecured Samba share with low-privileged access reveals the OT access credentials.
- The domain controller can also be accessed from the admin directly by SSH (using the creds found on the web page)
- The domain controller's smb.conf and samba/private files are compressed and moved through corpadmin, back to the firewall
- The compressed files remain an indicator of compromise so are deleted when moved off a host.
- The compressed files are sent to the attacker from the edge firewall via netcat
- the attacker sets up an environment to perform offline cracking, determines the accounts stored in the domain controller and uses hashcat to brute-force a collision
- Newly armed with OT credentials, the attacker moves laterally, back to the corpadmin host and then to the jumpbox through the it/ot bridge
- from the jumpbox, the attacker is able to use information found in local logs and from the unsecured fileserver to access and modify operational technology assets.
- this can be confirmed by accessing the human/machine interface.
